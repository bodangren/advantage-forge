# Product guidelines

Use the established chibi fantasy style across models, sprites, and games.

## Asset clarity

- Establish the silhouette before surface detail.
- Compare front, side, back, and three-quarter views.
- Keep scale, palette, and materials consistent within each kit.
- Review faces and equipment at the actual sprite size.
- Use the existing Forge asset skill for category-specific review.

## Game behavior

- Keep learning evidence independent of speed rewards.
- Treat setbacks as recovery or rest in the legacy rewrites.
- Keep touch targets readable and usable on small screens.
- Verify the same learning outcome in both renderers.
- Preserve the documented Potion Rush score and XP distinction.

## Documentation

- Start status reports with the current result and next action.
- Use one word for one meaning.
- Limit descriptive sentences to 25 words.
- Limit procedural sentences to 20 words.
- Limit descriptive paragraphs to six sentences.
- Preserve exact source quotations, commands, schemas, and paths.
- State uncertainty and evidence limits explicitly.
