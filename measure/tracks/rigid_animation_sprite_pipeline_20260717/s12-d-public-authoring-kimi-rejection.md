Kimi rejected S12-D in eight visual categories:

1. The head and cheeks are too narrow and tall for the approved round child
   proportion.
2. The helmet does not read as the target domed helmet with broad brim, rear
   ridge, shield/flame crest, and studs.
3. The hair lacks readable front fringe curls, side hair, and a coherent back
   hair cap.
4. The face reads as square dots rather than oval eyes with subtle nose, mouth,
   and ears.
5. The costume lacks the target scarf/collar, shaped tunic, sleeve cuffs, hem,
   wrap belt, and three readable pouches.
6. The upper and lower arms do not read as connected anatomy, and the hands are
   undersized blocks rather than rounded hands.
7. The lower body does not clearly separate shorts/leggings from cuffed boots
   with soles.
8. Back detail and the side silhouette are too thin and insubstantial to match
   the approved turnaround.
