acceptance, final-art acceptance, a theme-pack artifact, animation evidence, or

authoritative: transform-only compacting cannot establish the intended visual

## Rejected visual approach

The final styling revision shortened the body vertically, widened it in X, and
deepened it in Z while enlarging the head and helmet. It improved occupied
bounds from 19-pixel-wide E/W silhouettes to 42-pixel-wide E/W silhouettes and
kept all frames inside their 128x128 cells. That dimensional improvement did
not prove visual-identity convergence and does not override the owner's
rejection.

No approved visual target existed before modeling. Consequently the run could
only optimize generic compactness and readability, not fidelity to an accepted
character design. The preliminary Kimi review of completeness and clipping is
retained as diagnostic evidence but is superseded as an acceptance decision.

## Required next workflow

Before another novel character is modeled:

1. Generate a provenance-bound character turnaround/reference target covering
   at minimum front, three-quarter, side, and back views. A reduced view set is
   allowed only when the owner explicitly approves it.
2. Record the generator/provider, prompt/inputs, output identity, provenance,
   and originality review. No provider is currently approved or callable by
   this track; do not silently use MMX or another image generator.
3. Obtain explicit owner approval of the reference target before 3D modeling.
4. Iterate the semantic 3D assembly against that target through supported
   public MCP operations.
5. Capture side-by-side Kimi review across the approved views and preserve the
   full revision lineage until visual convergence is explicitly accepted.

Later pose authoring may snapshot many static rigid poses efficiently, but that
future work does not turn static directions into temporal animation. Clips,
timing, interpolation, playback, and atlases remain the blocked rigid-animation
track's responsibility.
