## Proportion measurements

I marked landmarks on pixel grids from the JPGs and the Kim PNG with Python 3 and Pillow. I used the front panels of the old `out/sprout-*/render.png` sheets. I checked palm direction from the front and side where both views show open hands. Ben's panel only shows a hand. I rendered the saved Ben GLB at the same camera angles in `/tmp` for the full-body values.

Each measurement cell uses this order:

- **Body:** total height in heads, arm length ÷ torso height, leg share, head width ÷ shoulder width.
- **Reach:** wrist and fingertip positions as fractions of the hip-to-knee distance. The hip is 0, mid-thigh is 0.50, and the knee is 1. Negative means above the hip.
- **Face:** eye line from crown ÷ head height, eye opening height ÷ head height, pupil spacing ÷ head width, eye opening width ÷ face width, pupil spacing ÷ face width.
- **Landmarks:** measure from the crown to the chin and soles. Exclude hats, buns, tails, loose hair tips and cast shadows. Take the widest head span above the eye line and the widest cheek span without ears or hair. Average the visible eye openings, without lashes or glasses. Use the shoulder centers and the inferred hip joints. Measure the arm straight from shoulder to fingertip in the image.

**Confidence:** Visible heights and clear palm views are high (about ±5 px on drawings or Kim, ±3 px on old panels). Face ratios are medium. Shoulder, hip, knee and reach values are estimates (about ±20 px on drawings or Kim, ±8 px on old panels). Skirts hide the hip and sometimes the knee; those estimates are low. The old Forge front camera is 8° above level. These are image ratios, not joint lengths.

The [LATO.2 README](https://github.com/LoHhhha/LATO.2) specifies CUDA and about 8 GB VRAM. The Radeon HD 6450 cannot run it. The [free demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/0x4c48/lato2-mesh-generation) is the only free path here. The [code uses MIT](https://github.com/LoHhhha/LATO.2/blob/master/LICENSE). The demo accepts one image or one mesh. For Kim, use one full-body front view on a plain background, not the sheet. The [image code](https://huggingface.co/spaces/0x4c48/lato2-mesh-generation/blob/main/src/utils/image.py) flattens alpha to white and limits the longer side to 1,024 px. It removes the background and makes a 518 × 518 crop. The demo exports triangle-only GLB and OBJ files without UVs, textures or skin weights. The vertex target is 200–5,000, with 2,000 as the default. The actual count can differ.

**GCP option, not selected:** The PM reports quota in `reading-advantage`. Quota does not guarantee Spot capacity. Daniel must approve the estimated USD 1–2 spend before the trial. Allow 1–2 hours for setup through deletion, excluding compatibility work. The [image script](https://github.com/LoHhhha/LATO.2/blob/master/scripts/image_e2e_inference.py) uses BF16. The T4 [lacks native BF16](https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda/gpus). Confirm a T4-compatible build before provisioning. An L4 supports native BF16, but needs a separate estimate and approval.

The [LATO.2 weights](https://huggingface.co/0x4c48/LATO.2/tree/main) use MIT and total 3.58 GB. The image route downloads about 6.5 GB of weights including TRELLIS, BiRefNet and DINOv2. TRELLIS and BiRefNet list MIT. DINOv2 uses Apache-2.0. Review the pinned licenses and model code before use. Spot preemption can interrupt the download or trial. An idle VM still incurs charges.

1. Select a [T4 zone](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/regions-zones/gpu-regions-zones) in `asia-southeast1`, such as `asia-southeast1-a`.
2. After approval, create one Spot T4 VM from a [CUDA Deep Learning VM image](https://docs.cloud.google.com/deep-learning-vm/docs/images). Use one temporary boot disk.
3. Clone `LoHhhha/LATO.2`. Install the pinned dependencies. Fetch the weights with `python scripts/ckpt_download.py`.
4. Run `scripts/image_e2e_inference.py` on one approved image. Copy the resulting OBJ out. Confirm the copy opens.
5. **Required last step:** Delete the VM and boot disk in the same session, even after a failed trial: `gcloud compute instances delete "$TRIAL_VM" --project=reading-advantage --zone="$TRIAL_ZONE" --delete-disks=boot`. Use the trial's exact name and zone. Verify the VM and disk are gone.

**Demo test:** The [public armor example](https://github.com/LoHhhha/LATO.2/blob/master/assets/example_image/80ad7988fc2ce62fc655b21a8950865566ec3f5a8b4398f2502db6414a3e6834.webp) returned 2,652 vertices and 4,986 triangles in 19.9 seconds at seed 42. The mesh kept the broad silhouette and thin halo but merged the gauntlet fingers into a coarse grip. It had 322 boundary edges and 24 non-manifold edges. I saved the GLB, OBJ and preview under `/tmp/sprout-redesign-20261123/lato2/`. The run used no login or project art.

The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10623v1) reports thin detail in geometry-conditioned examples. It does not establish that one front image preserves human proportions, separated fingers or palm direction. The output has no guarantee of bilateral symmetry or clean loops at joints. The demo preview hides inconsistent face winding. Use `k.add` to display a static reference. It does not add UVs or bind a mesh to the rig. For mesh-to-SDF, repair and close the mesh before conversion. Keep the SDF pick. Cloud hosting does not address the geometry or rig risks. Use a generated mesh only to compare large forms after it passes Kim's front and side checks.

The old `muse/elements/lily-ref-render.png` matches `out/sprout-lily/render.png` pixel for pixel. Its top panel includes the drawing. The void run did not test a 3D-only reference. For a future call, use a clean `--no-ref` render, not the compare sheet. No paid call until the PM approves the trial.

## First trial: Mom on Kim's base

Pick the new SDF base. It gives the song and game teams the same character at repeatable angles. Mom is the faster 3D trial because Kim supplies front and side anatomy, including hands. Mom's drawing supplies her identity. Lily has no side reference.

1. Make one rest-pose front and side view of Mom. Place them beside Kim at the same scale. Place Mom's drawing beside them for the identity check.
2. Use level orthographic front and side views for the trial. Apply the tolerances below to the front view with the same landmark definitions.
3. Check both hands. In front, the hands sit at the sides and show a narrow edge, not the palm. In profile, the thumbs point forward and the backs of the hands face out. Apply this rule to every humanoid rest pose.
4. After the shape passes, review one song cutout at 900 px or taller and one idle/walk sprite sample at 128 px. Stop for PM review before another character.
