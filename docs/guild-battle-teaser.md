# Chibi Quest: The Great Battle (teaser storyboards)

**Version:** 1.2
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** Approved for production (owner decisions of 2026-09-30)
**Track:** [showcase_battle_teaser_20260930](../measure/tracks/showcase_battle_teaser_20260930/)

A 45-second teaser: the enemy horde, the heroes, the charge, and the crash. There are two
versions with one shared choreography: 16:9 (1920 x 1080) for Facebook and YouTube, and 9:16
(1080 x 1920) for Reels and Shorts.

## Owner decisions

- Chibi Quest is always for **Primary Advantage** and **Tutor Advantage**. The end card shows
  those two products. It never names Reading Advantage as the product.
- Numbers on screen: **68 kinds of enemies** and **15 heroes**. The necromancer is an enemy,
  not a hero. The horde shows every enemy model that has a GLB, so at least 68 kinds are on
  screen. A color variant is a recolor and does not count as a kind. (The count was 54 at
  first; the owner raised it to 68 on 2026-09-30.)
- Music: the Reading Advantage jingle (`advantage-pr/assets/video-assets/music/reading-advantage-jingle.mp3`).
- Voice: `mmx speech`, voice `Thai_Optimistic_girl`, model `speech-2.8-hd`, female particles only.
- Light: never put the sun behind the subject. Each army shot has its key light behind the
  camera. Warm (heroes) and cold (horde) come from color, not from the sun position.

## The battlefield

- An open valley, about 90 m by 60 m. The battle line runs east to west.
- **East (the horde):** dead trees, ruins, gravestones, watchfires, and a rock where the fire
  dragon waits. Cool light and purple fog.
- **West (the heroes):** a low hill for the archers, tents, banners, and the hamlet's cottages
  and windmill behind. Warm golden light.
- **The center:** a banner beside the battle line.
- **Horde:** about 100 enemies on the ground, sorted by height. The smallest (goblins,
  kobolds, rats) stand in front and the tallest (golems, ogres, trolls) stand at the back.
  Ghosts, wraiths, and specters float above the ranks. Gargoyles, bats, and imps fly higher.
- **Heroes:** 50 heroes (15 classes in their color variants). Melee in front, support in the
  middle, archers and mages on the hill.
- **Leaders:** the knight (heroes) and the orc warlord (horde) stand in front of their lines
  and meet first.

## Files and commands

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `battle.html`, `src/showcase/battle/main.ts` | The page: the battlefield, the armies, and `seek(t)`. `?format=9x16` gives the vertical version. |
| `src/showcase/battle/script.ts` | The shared choreography and the two shot lists. |
| `src/showcase/battle/roster.json` | Generated: every hero and enemy model with its size, clips, and presets. |
| `scripts/battle.ts` | Roster, stills, the recorder, and the thumbnail. |
| `thumb.html`, `src/showcase/battle/thumb.ts`, `thumb.css` | The thumbnail dressing over one still (`?bg=`). |
| `scripts/battle-audio.ts` | The soundtrack: jingle sections, the Thai voice-over, and sound effects. |

```bash
node --import tsx scripts/battle.ts roster                     # src/showcase/battle/roster.json
node --import tsx scripts/battle-audio.ts voice                # out/battle/vo-*.mp3 (mmx)
node --import tsx scripts/battle-audio.ts                      # out/battle/soundtrack.wav
node --import tsx scripts/battle.ts frames 16x9 3 8 30 36      # stills
node --import tsx scripts/battle.ts record 16x9                # out/battle/chibi-quest-battle-16x9.mp4
node --import tsx scripts/battle.ts record 9x16                # out/battle/chibi-quest-battle-9x16.mp4
# The thumbnail: a 3D still (heavy: the full scene, about 4 min), then the dressing (a few seconds).
node --import tsx scripts/battle.ts frames 16x9 36.15 '?cam=-0.4,0.95,3.6,0.1,1.3,0,34&turn=30,65'
cp out/battle/frames/16x9-t036.15.png out/battle/thumb-bg-16x9.png
node --import tsx scripts/battle.ts thumb 16x9 '?bg=/out/battle/thumb-bg-16x9.png'   # out/battle/thumb-16x9.jpg
node --import tsx scripts/battle.ts thumb 9x16 '?bg=/out/battle/thumb-bg-16x9.png'   # out/battle/thumb-9x16.jpg (same still)
```
