2. Head: hair as a cap swept back with 6 to 8 locks, and the braid as a `sdf.chain` of 9 segments (r 0.04 to 0.025) from the left shoulder down to y 0.3, with a twist groove in bump; the circlet (a torus R 0.15 r 0.006 at y 0.74); big eyes, rosy cheek discs, a small smile; round human ears.
3. Body: the gold collar (a shell ring at the neck); the cream front panel (a flat box 0.12 wide from the collar to the hem) with gold trim strokes in paint; the belt with a gold clasp disc and the green gem sphere; a chain (5 small tori) from the clasp to the book.
4. Held items: the book (a box 0.09 x 0.11 x 0.03) on `hand.L`, held at the chest; the spirit (a sphere r 0.045 with two eye discs and four flat leaf wings 0.05 long) on the `spirit` bone above the open right palm, the right arm forward with the palm up. Attack is the summon flare; a second clip is a spirit dash forward and back if the base has a second attack. `./forge check summoner` must end with `result ok` and the ground check must be ok.
5. Sprites: the violet braid, the green robe with gold, the book, and the glowing spirit must read at 128 px. The spirit must stay green, not white.
