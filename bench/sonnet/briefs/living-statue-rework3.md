Keep the rig, the clips, the sword, the shield with its rings and boss, the mask with the glowing eyes, the crown ring and garland band, the skirt, and the pauldrons. Change the surface, not the shapes, except where noted.

Marble, largest first:
1. Base color and veins. The stone is a warm off-white with grey-blue veins, not a flat grey. Set every marble body to base #e4e0d8 with a paintFn that adds veins: `mixRgb(base, vein, v)` where `v = clamp01((noise.fbm(x * 9 + 3, y * 3, z * 9, 3) - 0.35) * 4)` and the vein color is #8a8e9a, plus a second finer set at 22x with vein #b8b4ae and weight 0.5. Vary the vein direction per body (rotate the sample coordinates 30 to 60 degrees on the helm, the pauldrons, and the shield) so the pieces read as separate blocks. Keep the down-facing shading gradient (the crevices #8e8a82), but as a soft gradient, not a hard step. All the marble bodies share one helper so the veining is consistent.
2. Surface finish. Marble is polished: roughness 0.35, metalness 0, on every marble body; the visor slot, the seams, and the skirt grooves stay rough (0.7) and dark (#5e5a52). Add a fine grain in bump: `bump: (x, y, z) => 0.0004 * noise.fbm(x * 90, y * 90, z * 90, 2)`, and keep the six hand-placed cracks as painted #6a665e lines with a 0.002 m groove. Chip two edges: one on the shield rim and one on a pauldron corner (subtracted spheres r 0.015) so the breaks read as stone.
3. Tone separation for sprites. The 128 px sprites must show the helm, the mask, the pauldrons, the skirt, and the shield as separate tones. Give the helm and the shield slightly cooler veining (#7a8090) and the body slightly warmer (#9a948a); paint the inside of every recess #56524c. The eyes stay #6ad0ff emissive 1.4 on a #103050 base.
4. Helm dome. Restore a rounded dome 0.13 m tall under the crown ring (an ellipsoid 0.22 x 0.26 x 0.22 cut flat 0.11 m above the band), with the ring and the garland on top; no flat bowl, no spiky rim. Keep the gem.

Check every change in out/living-statue/render.png and out/living-statue/sprites/preview.png: the surface must read as veined polished marble in the three-quarter view and the sprites must show separate tones. Report the three largest remaining differences.

Limits: under 65,000 triangles. No `warning:` lines. `./forge check living-statue` must end with `result ok` and the ground line must be ok. Set `FORGE_WORKERS=2` on every forge command. Never commit. Only edit assets/living-statue.ts. Finish with one `./forge all living-statue`.
