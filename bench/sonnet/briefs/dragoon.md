# dragoon (heroes/martial/dragoon) -> assets/dragoon.ts

A young dragoon (lancer knight) hero about 1.0 m to the helm crest, the lance above that, faces +Z, on the knight base (assets/knight.ts: the rogue's body, face, and chibi skeleton with knee bones, `cloak` and `plume` bones; a sword rigid in `hand.R`, a shield rigid on the left forearm; clips idle, walk, run, attack, attack2, hit, death, victory; variant slots eyes, hair, skin, clothing with presets). Bar 8/10 (character). Game: Dragon Rider (the player is a lancer), so the lance and the dragon-wing helm are the focal props.

Mockup: docs/hero-mockups/dragoon_001.jpg (set `reference` to that path). Match its idea, with one change: no beard; every hero on this set has the young, round, beardless face. Dark blue steel plate: a rounded helm with a riveted silver brim, a tall center spike and two curved horn blades on top, and two small dragon wings on the sides of the helm; an open face with dark brown hair fringe under the brim, a serious brow, big eyes; layered dragon-scale pauldrons with a silver edge; a blue cuirass with a silver dragon-crest emblem; a segmented tasset skirt; a silver-studded belt with a diamond buckle; blue gauntlets; a short red cape at the back; blue greaves and boots; a long steel lance (1.5 m, a leaf-shaped head, a ring guard) held upright in the right hand, the butt on the ground; the left hand a gauntlet fist at the side; no shield.

Palette: plate dark blue #3a4a6a with #26334a shadow (metalness 0.7, roughness 0.45); silver trim and brim #c3c8cf (metalness 0.8, roughness 0.35); rivets #d8dce0; hair #4a2e1c; skin #f2c7a4; cape red #c93a32 with #8a2420 folds (roughness 0.85); leather straps #4e2d1c; lance shaft #4a4f55, head and guard #c3c8cf; boots #26334a.
Variants (the hero slot set): eyes (brown default, blue #2f6aa8, green #3d7a35), hair (brown default, black #231a17, blond #c4974a), skin (fair default, tan #d49a72, brown #8a5a3e), armor (blue default, black #2a2a30, crimson #7a2a2a, green #2f5a3a). Presets: default, black, crimson.

Construction recipe:
1. Copy assets/knight.ts. Remove the shield, the shield face, the sword, the plume feathers, the scarf, and the tabard. Keep the rig, the clips, the helm (reshape), the cuirass, the pauldrons (reshape as scales), the mail, the gauntlets, the belt, the tassets, the leggings, the greaves, the cape (shorten to the waist).
2. Head: the helm dome with a riveted silver brim (a torus R 0.15 r 0.012 with 8 rivet spheres), a center spike (a cone 0.12 tall), two horn blades (flat curved extrusions 0.1 tall at x +-0.09), two dragon wings (flat extruded wing outlines 0.12 wide with three finger ridges) on the sides at y 0.8 sweeping back; the open face with a hair fringe under the brim; a serious brow.
3. Body: pauldrons as three overlapping scale plates each (rounded boxes, smoothUnion 0.008) with a silver edge; a silver dragon emblem (an extruded stroke) on the cuirass; the tasset skirt as 6 segmented plates; a silver-studded belt with a diamond buckle; a short cape from the shoulders to y 0.3.
4. Held item: the lance (a shaft r 0.014, 1.5 m tall, with a ring guard torus at 0.5 m and a leaf head 0.18 tall) rigid on `hand.R`, held upright beside the right shoulder with the butt at y 0.0 in idle and at least 0.04 m clear of the helm wings in every clip. Attack is a forward lance thrust (both hands, the lance tilts to 20 degrees above horizontal); attack2 is a jump strike (a crouch, a hop with the lance pointed down 45 degrees, a landing). `./forge check dragoon` must end with `result ok` and the ground check must be ok.
5. Sprites: the winged helm, the blue plate, the red cape, and the upright lance must read at 128 px. The face must show under the brim.

Limits: under 65,000 triangles, `detail` 0.004 on the face, 0.006 elsewhere. No `warning:` lines. Set `FORGE_WORKERS=2` on every forge command. Never commit. Only create or edit assets/dragoon.ts. Finish with one `./forge all dragoon`.
