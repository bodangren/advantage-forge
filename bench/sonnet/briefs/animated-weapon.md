# animated-weapon (enemies/construct/animated-weapon) -> assets/animated-weapon.ts

A floating haunted sword and shield with no body, about 1.0 m tall from the mist to the pommel, faces +Z, on the animated armor base (assets/animated-armor.ts: the construct rig with a weapon hand; clips idle, walk, run, attack, hit, death, awaken). Bar 8/10 (character). Most of the base body is removed; only bones stay.

Mockup: docs/enemy-mockups/animated-weapon_001.jpg (set `reference` to that path). Match its idea: a big dark iron longsword standing upright, point down, hovering above the ground with a bright violet rune line glowing down the center of the blade, a ribbed grip and a round pommel, a violet magic coil (a beaded spiral) wrapped around the blade below the guard; a small round bronze shield with three rivets and a white eye with a black pupil at its center hovering beside the sword on the left; a bank of pale lilac mist at the ground with violet glow wisps around the sword tip.

Palette: blade #4a4a52 with lit #6a6a74 (metalness 0.7, roughness 0.4); rune line #c060ff emissive 2.2; grip #3a3a40; pommel #5a5a62; coil #9a4aa8 with a #c070d0 lit and emissive 0.4; shield #b87a3a bronze (metalness 0.7) with rivets #6a4a2a, eye #f2f2f2 with a #101014 pupil; mist #d8cfe8 opacity 0.7 with violet #b070ff emissive tips.
Variants: rune (violet default, cyan #40e0ff, red #ff4040), shield (bronze default, silver #b8bcc4, black #2a2a30).

Construction recipe:
1. Copy assets/animated-armor.ts. Keep the skeleton but remove every armor body. Build: the sword on the `chest` bone (blade: an extruded elongated blade profile 0.55 long, 0.09 wide, point down, tip at y 0.18; guard: a rounded box 0.2 x 0.03 x 0.05 with two upturned ends; grip: a cylinder r 0.02 h 0.14 with ribs in bump; pommel: a sphere r 0.035), the rune line (a thin emissive box 0.008 x 0.45 x 0.004 on both blade faces), the magic coil (an sdf.chain spiral of 12 beads r 0.018 around the blade below the guard, on the `chest` bone), the shield on the `hand.L` bone at x 0.18, y 0.55 (a disc r 0.11 with a rim torus, three rivets, an eye: a white disc r 0.035 with a black pupil sphere), the mist at the ground (a smoothUnion of 8 flattened ellipsoids from x -0.2 to 0.2 at y 0.04, opacity 0.7, and four wisp chains rising to y 0.25 with violet emissive tips) on the `hips` bone; the mist touches y 0 so the asset stands on the ground plane.
2. Clips: idle bobs the sword (hips move y 0.02 sine) and rocks the shield; walk drifts with a stronger bob and the mist trailing (scale the mist chain bones); attack thrusts the sword forward and down (chest rotate x 60) then returns; hit jolts back; death drops the sword flat on the ground (chest rotate x 90, move y down to the mist) and the shield falls; awaken lifts the sword from a flat pose to upright. No held items (the sword is on the chest bone). The ground check must be ok.
3. Sprites: the glowing rune line, the shield eye, and the mist must read at 128 px.

Limits: under 40,000 triangles, `detail` 0.004 on the shield eye, 0.006 elsewhere. No `warning:` lines. Set `FORGE_WORKERS=2` on every forge command. Never commit. Only create or edit assets/animated-weapon.ts. Finish with one `./forge all animated-weapon`.
