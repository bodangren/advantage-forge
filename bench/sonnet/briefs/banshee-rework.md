Mockup: docs/enemy-mockups/banshee_001.jpg (already the `reference`). Keep the rig, the clips, the variants, the arms, and the eyes. Rebuild the hair, the head scale, the gown, and the wisps.

The hair concept must change. Thin chain locks read as bare twigs at 128 px. Build the hair as ONE streaming mass, then add a few thick locks on top:
1. Hair mass: a smoothUnion (k 0.03) of five to seven large tapered shapes that sweep up and back from the scalp like a flame blown upward: each an ellipsoid about 0.12 x 0.3 x 0.08 rotated 15 to 35 degrees back and fanned across the head (x from -0.2 to +0.2), their bases inside the skull, tops reaching y 1.15 to 1.25. Add a scalp cap (an ellipsoid 1.08x the head, cut at the face) under the mass. Displace the mass with `displace(0.012, fbm)` for waviness. Paint #efeae0 with a #cfc8b8 shade in the crevices (paintFn by noise) and strand grooves in `bump` with a vertical frequency (y * 4, x * 30). Then add six thick locks (`sdf.chain`, r 0.03 to 0.01, 0.35 long) that curl out from the mass edge for silhouette interest. The side view must show hair covering the whole back of the head. The hair must read as a white mane in every sprite direction.
2. Head scale: the head is too small. Scale the head, face, eyes, mouth, and hair mass so the head is 0.26 tall (about one third of the 0.8 m body without the hair). Move the eyes and mouth with it.
3. Gown: a revolve profile with a real skirt: r 0.09 at the neck (y 0.66), r 0.13 at the chest (y 0.55), r 0.17 at the waist (y 0.42), then flaring to r 0.32 at the hem (y 0.12). Displace with `displace(0.03, (x,y,z) => noise.fbm(x*5, y*2, z*5, 2))` for deep vertical folds, and cut a ragged hem with eight subtracted wedges of different depths. Add the wrapped collar (three stacked displaced tori) and the mockup's cyan drip marks (five small emissive ellipsoids on the front of the skirt).
4. Wisps: four flat ribbons (chain scaled [1, 0.3, 1], width 0.06 to 0.02) that leave the hem at the sides and back, curl outward and up to y 0.35, opacity 0.6, gown color with a cyan emissive tip. They are thinner and more transparent than now. One or two reach y 0 so the asset stands on the ground plane.
