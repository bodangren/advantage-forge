# banshee rework 2 (enemies/undead/banshee) -> assets/banshee.ts

Rework the existing assets/banshee.ts (a fresh agent; the file builds, ground ok, no held items). Bar 8/10 (character). The last review scored 7.3. Mockup: docs/enemy-mockups/banshee_001.jpg (already the `reference`). Keep the rig, the clips, the variants, the arms, the eyes, the gown, and the collar. Rebuild the head scale, the hair, and the wisps.

The three review issues, largest first:
1. Head scale. The head and face are small at 128 px, so the scream and the teeth do not read. Scale the skull, the face, the eyes, the mouth, and the hair anchor so the head is 0.27 m tall (about one third of the 0.8 m body without the hair). Move the eyes, the brows, and the mouth with the skull. Make the open mouth a dark ellipsoid cut 0.06 x 0.05 with a light teeth strip (a thin box 0.05 x 0.008) at the top edge. Keep the neck joint where the rig expects it.
2. Hair. The hair mass is a smooth scalloped shell with horn-like locks. The mockup has many thin wavy strands streaming up and back. Build it in two layers: (a) a thin cap 1.06x the skull, cut at the face, that covers the whole back of the head; (b) 20 to 30 medium `sdf.chain` locks (four points each, r 0.018 at the root to 0.006 at the tip, 0.3 to 0.45 m long) that start inside the cap and sweep up and back, fanned across the head from x -0.2 to +0.2 and tilted 15 to 40 degrees back, each with a gentle S wave (offset the middle points by 0.03 m sideways in alternate directions). Blend them with smoothUnion k 0.015 so they merge at the roots but separate at the tips. Paint #efeae0 with #cfc8b8 in the crevices (paintFn by noise) and give the locks a strand groove in bump along their length. No fan blades, no horns. The side and back sprites must show a white mane, not a helmet.
3. Wisps. The wisps are plain tubes. Make each a flat ribbon: a chain scaled [1, 0.3, 1] (width 0.06 at the hem to 0.02 at the tip) that twists (rotate each successive point 25 degrees about its own axis by building the ribbon from four short flattened segments with different Y rotations). Opacity 0.6, gown color on the outside, and an emissive inner face: a second thinner ribbon inside it, cyan #3ad0e0, emissive 1.5, dark base #106070 so it does not clip to white. Four ribbons: two from the sides, two from the back; one or two reach y 0 so the asset stands on the ground plane.

Check every fix in out/banshee/render.png and out/banshee/sprites/preview.png. Report the three largest remaining differences.

Limits: under 60,000 triangles. No `warning:` lines. `./forge check banshee` ground line must be ok. Set `FORGE_WORKERS=2` on every forge command. Never commit. Only edit assets/banshee.ts. Finish with one `./forge all banshee`.
