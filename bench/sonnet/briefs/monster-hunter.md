# monster-hunter (heroes/support/monster-hunter) -> assets/monster-hunter.ts

Mockup: docs/hero-mockups/monster-hunter_001.jpg (set `reference` to that path). Match its idea, with one change: no beard; every hero on this set has the young, round, beardless face.
