# fighter (heroes/martial/fighter) -> assets/fighter.ts

A young disciplined fighter hero about 1.0 m to the top of the helmet, faces +Z, on the knight base (assets/knight.ts: the rogue's body, face, and chibi skeleton with knee bones, `cloak` and `plume` bones; a sword rigid in `hand.R`, a shield rigid on the left forearm; clips idle, walk, run, attack, attack2, hit, death, victory; variant slots eyes, hair, skin, clothing with presets). Bar 8/10 (character).

Mockup: docs/hero-mockups/fighter_001.jpg (set `reference` to that path). Match its idea, with one change: no beard; every hero on this set has the young, round, beardless face. A simple steel skull cap with a brow band and a nasal bar over thick black hair; a calm face with big eyes; a blue cloth shoulder cape and collar; a chainmail shirt (a mail texture in bump and paint) to the hip; a leather chest strap with a brass buckle and a leather belt with a brass buckle; a blue cloth skirt panel under the mail; leather bracers and dark gloves; a round wooden buckler with a brass rim and a pointed boss on the left forearm; a plain arming sword with a brass guard held level in the right hand; dark boots.

Palette: helmet steel #bcc2cb with #8a9098 shadow (metalness 0.8, roughness 0.4); hair #231a17; skin #f2c7a4; cape and collar blue #3f6a9a with #2a4a70 folds; mail #8a9098 with #5a6068 rings (metalness 0.6, roughness 0.6); straps and belt #6b4226, buckles brass #c9a24a; skirt #3f6a9a; bracers #8a5a35, gloves #3a2a20; buckler face #8a5a35 with #6b4226 grain, rim and boss brass #c9a24a; sword blade #c3c8cf, guard and pommel #c9a24a, grip #3a2a20; boots #3a2a20.
Variants (the hero slot set): eyes (brown default, blue #2f6aa8, green #3d7a35), hair (black default, brown #4a2e1c, blond #c4974a), skin (fair default, tan #d49a72, brown #8a5a3e), clothing (blue default, red #c93a32, green #2e7a3c). Presets: default, red, green.

Construction recipe:
1. Copy assets/knight.ts. Remove the plume, the feathers, the crest, the heater shield, the tabard, and the pauldrons. Keep the rig, the clips, the cuirass (replace with the mail shirt), the belt, the tassets (replace with the skirt panel), the leggings, the greaves (shorten), the sword (reshape plain), the cape (shorten to a shoulder cape).
2. Head: the skull cap (a dome cut at the brow y 0.74 with a brow band torus and a nasal bar box 0.02 x 0.08 x 0.02 down the nose bridge); hair showing under the cap edge at the sides and the back; a calm mouth; round human ears.
3. Body: the mail shirt (the torso and the upper arms, a ring pattern in bump `0.0006 * sin(x*260)*sin(y*260)` and a two-tone ring paint); the shoulder cape (a rounded box over the shoulders to y 0.3 at the back) with a collar ring; the chest strap and belt with brass buckles; the skirt panel (a flat box 0.22 x 0.14 x 0.02 at the front); bracers and gloves.
4. Held items: the buckler (a disc r 0.13, a brass rim torus, a cone boss) rigid on the left forearm bone as the knight's shield is; the arming sword (a blade 0.36 long, 0.05 wide, a straight brass guard 0.12 wide, a round pommel) rigid on `hand.R`, held level at the hip in idle. Attack is a diagonal slash; attack2 is a buckler bash. `./forge check fighter` must end with `result ok` and the ground check must be ok.
5. Sprites: the steel cap, the blue cape, the mail, the buckler, and the sword must read at 128 px. The face must show under the cap.

Limits: under 65,000 triangles, `detail` 0.004 on the face, 0.006 elsewhere. No `warning:` lines. Set `FORGE_WORKERS=2` on every forge command. Never commit. Only create or edit assets/fighter.ts. Finish with one `./forge all fighter`.
