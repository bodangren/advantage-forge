[View the four-view turnaround](./riven-goblin-warrior-turnaround.png).

This mockup targets `enemies/humanoid/goblin-warrior`. It shows one melee scout in the Riven Lands language. It is a visual reference for a later 3D source asset, not a finished model.

## Form and materials

- A wiry, hunched adult goblin has long ears, a hooked nose, lean limbs, and a narrow waist.
- The left ear has one nick. Keep that mark visible in all views.
- The outfit uses patched charcoal cloth, worn brown leather, and one tarnished bronze shoulder guard.
- A diagonal strap, one belt pouch, rough boots, and wrapped forearms give the silhouette detail.
- One short crude dagger is the removable equipment part. Its catalog ID is `equipment/melee-weapons/dagger`.
- The palette uses olive skin, dark earth cloth, brown leather, and bronze metal.

The four views use one character design and one baseline. The visible shape should read at game size. Keep the face, ears, shoulder guard, and dagger clear at 128 pixels. Use soft, color-matched edges and smooth shading. Avoid chibi proportions, gore, and realistic horror detail.

## Modeling notes

Make the body and clothing a reusable source model. Keep the dagger separate for equipment swaps. Place the character on the ground plane with the forge's forward direction. Add named pivots for the hands and shoulder. Review front, side, and back silhouettes before building animations or sprites.
